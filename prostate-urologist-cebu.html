    What is Enlarged Prostate (BPH)?

    A common condition in older men where the prostate enlarges, causing urinary symptoms.

    Common Symptoms

    Frequent or urgent urination, weak stream, difficulty starting, and waking often at night.

    Causes & Risk Factors

    Age-related hormone changes and prostate cell growth.

    How It Is Diagnosed

    PSA blood test, digital rectal exam, urinary flow test, and ultrasound assess prostate size and bladder function.

    Treatment Options at UroElite

    Medications, minimally invasive procedures (UroLift, HoLEP, laser therapy), or surgery depending on severity.

    When to See a Urologist

    If you wake multiple times at night to urinate, have a weak stream, or feel unable to fully empty your bladder, schedule a urology consultation to prevent bladder damage.
